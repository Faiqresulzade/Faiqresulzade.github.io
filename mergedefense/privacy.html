English

This policy explains how the mobile game Merge Defense (package com.faig.mergedefense, “the App”) handles information. The App is developed and published by Faig Rasulzade, an individual developer based in Baku, Azerbaijan (“we”, “us”). Contact: faigrasulzada@gmail.com.

1. Information we do not collect

The App has no user accounts, no sign-in and no chat. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, camera or microphone. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive or store personal data about you.

2. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress (eras, battles, coins, gems, cards, skills, runes, hero items and settings such as sound and vibration) is saved locally on your device. It never leaves the device and is deleted when you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows only optional rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC), which you choose to watch for an in-game reward (for example double coins, extra meat, gems or a wheel spin). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / app set ID);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks, viewing) and diagnostic data such as crash information of the ad SDK.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, under Google’s Privacy Policy. See also How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the list of ad technology providers.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): before any ad is requested, the App shows Google’s consent message (Google User Messaging Platform). You can accept, refuse or choose personalised or non-personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy inside the App. In the United States, applicable state privacy choices are offered through the same message.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. In-app purchases

The App has no real-money purchases. In-game currencies (coins, gems, potions) are earned only by playing and have no money value.

5. Analytics

The App does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs other than the advertising SDK described above.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with anyone. Data processed by Google for advertising is described in section 3.

7. Security and retention

Data exchanged with Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Locally saved game data stays on your device until you delete it. Data held by Google is kept according to Google’s retention policies.

8. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the App are limited to content suitable for teens. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights and deleting your data

Because we do not hold personal data on our side, there is nothing to delete on our servers. You can remove all App data by using Settings → Apps → Merge Defense → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the App. To manage data held by Google, use Google Account – Data & privacy and your device ad settings. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data; contact us at the address above and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always published on this page with its effective date.

11. Contact

Faig Rasulzade, Baku, Azerbaijan — faigrasulzada@gmail.com
