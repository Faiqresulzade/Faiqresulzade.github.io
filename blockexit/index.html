Block Exit!

Colour block sliding puzzle · Android

Slide colourful blocks across the board and send each one out through the door of its own colour. Plan your moves, beat the timer and clear thousands of hand-checked levels with spinners, lifts, ice, chains and more.

  • Free to play, with optional ads and a one-time “Remove ads” purchase
  • No account, no sign-in – your progress stays on your device
  • Available in English, Azərbaycan dili, Türkçe and Русский
